How to Create AI Videos for Free: Step-by-Step Guide

Below is a simple video creation process — how to make ai video for free from zero to publish.

01

Idea and Text

Formulate your video idea and target format (shorts, promo, explainer video). Write a short video script: hero, action, result. Prepare an ai prompt (video prompt) of 1–3 sentences with key details: style, location, angle, duration.

Example: "Aerial shot of a futuristic city at sunset, cinematic lighting, 5 seconds."

02

Choose Service

Determine your scenario — generate ai video from text (Runway, Luma, Pika, PixVerse), talking head (Synthesia, HeyGen), or marketing storytelling (InVideo AI). Cross-check our tables for limits (credits), video resolution, and watermarks to stay within free.

03

Generation

Insert your ai prompt or upload materials (image/clip) and start creation. Test 2–3 variations with slight prompt tweaks to improve composition and motion. For longer stories, break into scenes (video scene) of 5–8 seconds each.

"Break video into 5–8s scenes to stay within free tier limits." — TrueFan.ai (2025). Source
04

Editing

In the online ai video editor, trim video clips, add titles, logo, music, light video effects, and subtitles. If needed, apply translation (translate video) and alternative ai voiceover to expand reach. Check narrative timing and rhythm.

05

Export and Publish

Select video export for your platform (9:16 vertical for Shorts/Reels or 16:9 for YouTube), ensure bitrate/quality (quality video output), and upload (video upload) directly to your chosen platform. Save prompt/settings — this speeds up the next iteration.

Text-to-Video Generation: Best Free AI Tools

Text to video is when generative ai text transforms your video script into moving footage. You set an ai prompt (video prompt) "what should happen," the model synthesizes frames and animation, selects style, lighting, and camera movement.

On generator free tiers, the best performers are:

  • Runway Gen-3 — universal text-to-video with style and duration control; great for 4–8 second clips.
  • Luma Dream Machine — strong motion realism, from short dailies to promo teasers.
  • Pika 1.0 / PixVerse — fast results for social media, presets, and simple editing.

Tips: Write concrete prompts from text with scene, angle, and action; set duration, format (vertical/horizontal), and key attributes ("cinematic," "soft light," "slow pan"). Iteratively refine ai text and capture successful formulations in a prompt library.

"Prompts should use short sentences, simple language, ~150 words=1min video; structure hook-core message-call-to-action." — TrueFan.ai (2025). Source
Prompt Examples:
Marketing (promo): "Close-up product shot on rotating turntable, soft studio light, glossy reflections, 5s, 9:16, add bold CTA text at the end."
Education (explainer): "Minimal flat animation explaining how to reset a password, clean transitions, calm palette, 10s, 16:9, captions included."
YouTube intro: "Dynamic logo reveal with neon trails, fast camera push-in, 3s, 16:9, cinematic."

Almost always yes: video watermark is present on free. Exceptions include Fotor, Lumen5, FlexClip, Kapwing — which allow free HD export without watermark.

"Runway free: 125 credits for ~25s total; Veo 3: 6-8s/generation, editor stitches clips unlimited." — Adlook.me (2025). Source

Ai credit or video credits — internal currency: each generation deducts credits, sometimes depending on duration/resolution/retries. Credits refresh daily/monthly or are sold in packs.

Fully unlimited is rare and usually temporary. Look for promos, educational plans, or open betas. Combine multiple services to work around limits.
