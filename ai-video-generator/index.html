What Is an AI Video Generator and How Does It Work?

An AI video generator is an ai powered system for video generation that automates generating content and editing. Modern AI video generators in 2025 use diffusion models and transformers to convert text prompts into videos via noise denoising and frame sequencing.

Generation Stages:

1

Prompt Analysis

Text converted to embeddings via transformers, key elements extracted for video.

2

Script/Keyframe Creation

Sequence of keyframes formed with motion trajectories via motion priors.

3

Scene Generation

Diffusion models create frames in latent space, interpolate intermediates.

4

AI Voiceover

Speech synthesizer adds voice to scenes based on text.

5

Final Render

Decoding frames, assembly into MP4 with effects.

How to Create AI Video in 5 Steps

How We Test

We test each tool with the same 3 prompts: UGC 9:16 8s (handheld, creator vibe); Explainer 16:9 20s (polished corporate); Product 1:1 10s (close-up, branding). Last tested: January 2025.

1

Idea and Script

Define goal and audience. Brief script with thesis points and CTA improves render quality. Write scene-by-scene scripts ≤60s per scene with explicit visual cues.

2

Visuals from Text

Write precise video prompts with style, duration, key scenes, and mood.

Example Prompts:

"Handheld tracking shot of a man with a beer walking around a Fourth of July party with cinematic lighting"

"Cinematic sequence of a young woman in a coffee shop during sunset… Start with a close-up and slowly pull back… Style: warm tones, shallow depth of field"

3

Voiceover

Choose voice and tempo. Upload your own or use AI; specify pauses and emphasis so narration sounds natural.

4

Editing

After rough generation, edit video: trim excess, sync frames and sound, add transitions, logo, and subtitles.

5

Export

Check titles, metadata, and aspect ratio for platform. Export in required codec and resolution, then test on multiple devices.

Editing and Post-Production

Editing Capabilities in AI Video Editors

Most platforms include video editor with key features:

  • Trim, crop, speed control
  • Music and effects, volume balance
  • Titles and auto-subtitles, translation
  • Logo/text overlay, animated lower-thirds
  • Stock video and audio library
  • Presets for YouTube, Reels, Ads

Integration with NLE (Premiere/Resolve/AE)

Mini-guide on exports:

  • Codecs: Export ProRes (Mac/Premiere) or DNxHR (Windows/Resolve); H.264 for final delivery
  • LUT/color: Embed Rec.709 gamma; avoid flat/log unless grading in NLE
  • Subtitles: Export SRT (sidecar) or burn-in captions
  • Audio: Match LUFS (-14 for broadcast, -16 for social)
  • Typical issues: Variable FPS (VFR) from AI tools; convert to CFR in Handbrake or FFmpeg

Legal & Safety / Provenance

Commercial Rights

You retain rights to videos you create with most platforms (VEED, HeyGen, Synthesia). Avoid content infringing copyrights/trademarks. Check platform-specific ToS.

Stock Media Licensing

InVideo/Veed integrate iStock/Pexels/Unsplash. Higher tiers unlock premium libraries; verify commercial use rights per asset.

Voice Clone/Avatar Consent

Platforms require explicit consent for cloning voice/likeness. Upload signed release forms; follow GDPR/CCPA.

C2PA/Provenance

Google Veo 3.1 embeds SynthID/C2PA watermark in every frame. Runway/Sora support optional C2PA tagging. Enable in export settings.

Data Storage & Processing
