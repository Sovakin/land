OCR and AI Technology: How We Ensure Accuracy

What is OCR?
Optical Character Recognition (OCR) converts printed or handwritten text in images into machine-readable digital text. Traditional OCR analyzes pixel patterns and matches them to known character templates.

How AI improves OCR:
Modern AI-enhanced OCR uses convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to detect text regions, recurrent neural networks (RNNs/LSTMs) to understand character sequences, and Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC) to decode outputs without forced alignment.

Our Pipeline

  1. Upload & Preprocessing: Image alignment (deskew), binarization, noise reduction.
  2. Text Detection: YOLO-family detector identifies text blocks.
  3. Recognition: CNN extracts features → LSTM captures sequence → CTC decodes.
  4. AI Postprocessing: Language model validates word context (e.g., "O" vs. "0").
  5. Export: Clean text output in chosen format.

How to Get the Best OCR Results

Quality input = better output. Here's how to optimize your images:

1

Use High Resolution

Scan at 300 DPI minimum. Low-resolution images blur character edges, confusing OCR engines.

2

Control Lighting and Contrast

Shoot in even, diffuse lighting. Avoid shadows. Aim for balanced contrast between text and background.

3

Align and Crop

Straighten text to horizontal. Remove margins and background clutter. Crop to the text region only.

4

Minimize Noise

Clean scans beat dirty ones. Remove dust and artifacts. Use lossless formats (PNG, TIFF) when possible.
